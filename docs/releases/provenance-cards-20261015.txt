Source interrogation: compact provenance cards

Display retained source and documentary provenance as bordered metadata cards,
eight across on wide screens, four on tablets and two on mobile.
Nested field paths retain their context; arrays retain their item positions.
Original values, identifiers, rights and URLs remain available without truncation.
Long values scroll within keyboard-focusable fields rather than stretching rows.
No changes to research answers, retrieval, source identities or archival records.
